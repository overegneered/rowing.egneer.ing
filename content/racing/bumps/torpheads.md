Due to poor, but not disastrous, river conditions, [[torpids|Torpids]] 2026 was replaced with **TorpHeads** — a head race run on what would have been the Saturday of Torpids. The idea was twofold: it would allow for more crews to compete than would be possible under [[divisions#Half divisions|half divisions]], as was done in 2025; and would be safer and easier to run on high stream in comparison to [[bumps racing|Bumps]].

Owing to the success of TorpHeads 2026, it is likely it will run again in later years where the stream is high but still safely rowable.
