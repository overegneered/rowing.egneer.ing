---
title: Rowing On
---
**Rowing On** is a qualifying race for [[bumps racing|bumps regattas]] to decide who places in the [[divisions#Fixed and Rowing On divisions|Rowing On divisions]]. It takes place either on the Friday (with a contingency for Sunday[^1]) or Saturday before the main regatta for [[torpids|Torpids]] or [[summer eights|Eights]], respectively. Very few crews fail to qualify in most cases, particularly [[summer eights|Eights]] with its extra division —  normally it's around 3 per side.

The course is shorter than that of bumps itself, running from Longbridges to the Cox Stone, though OURCs may choose to shorten it in averse conditions. It is the same course as the [[tamesis regatta|Tamesis Regatta]]).

[^1]: Torpids requires a contingency due to the much higher likelihood of the Friday having to be abandoned due to inclement conditions.
