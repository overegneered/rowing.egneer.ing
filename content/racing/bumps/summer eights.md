**Summer Eights**, also **Summer VIIIs** (or just **Eights** or **VIIIs**), is a [[bumps racing|bumps race]] run in Trinity (Summer) Term Wednesday–Saturday in fifth week (with [[rowing on|rowing on]] the preceding Saturday). It is the culmination of the college rowing year and the most prestigious and spectated college rowing event. In fact, it gets so busy that on the Saturday of Eights that [[bankriders|bankriders]] are forbidden due to danger to pedestrians on the towpath.

## Differences to [[torpids|Torpids]]

Eights differs from Torpids in a few main ways:

- It has seven [[divisions|divisions]] instead of six;
- [[bumping|Bumped]] crews stop rowing;
- There are more supporters (especially on Saturday); and
- It's warmer.

Torpids is also far likelier to be cancelled or run under a contingency.
